# Surface: Choco Makes one-page shop (index.html)

**Mode:** Persuade. A visitor who met Choco Makes at a market (or never did) decides what to order and sends an order sheet to the maker.

**Audience / job:** university students, men and women. Browse current small-batch stock, see honest counts, add items with variants, register interest in sold-out pieces, send a numbered order sheet. Owner keeps it current via `data/shop.json`.

**Sections:** header nav · intro with one feature panel · shop grid · updates (dated notes) · about · footer. Order sheet docks at the bottom, unfolds upward.

**Constraints:** no invented products, prices, reviews or policies; placeholders are labelled. CAD. Web3Forms access key supplied by owner. Post-order confirmation copy is the owner's (placeholder). WCAG 2.2 AA.

**Unresolved:** pickup/delivery/payment; domain; real photos and inventory; Web3Forms key; confirmation copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet stationery boutique where the pastels are bookbinding cloth, and ordering is a numbered carbon-copy order slip that docks small at the bottom edge and unfolds into the full sheet. It refuses the pastel-gradient cutesy shop and the big always-open cart sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: cool paper ground #F5F4F7, charcoal ink #25232C. Lilac, pink and light-blue linen-weave book cloth appear as surfaces behind products, in one intro panel, and as one pink swatch carrying the About facts label (added in the finish review so the lower page is not washed out), never as page fill. Tipped-on paper labels carry names. A literary serif display voice over a plain grotesk UI voice. Square corners, hairline rules, tinted duplicate copies stacked under the slip. Charcoal solid buttons.

STORY: Small-batch handmade pieces, counted honestly; pick a few, send a sheet straight to the person who made them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: header (serif wordmark left, Shop/Updates/About right); 5:7 split, serif headline + charcoal "Browse the shop" left, lilac cloth feature panel with tipped label right; top of the 4-column cloth-backed product grid; slim slip docked at the bottom: "Order sheet No. ####" · items · total CAD · Review & send.

FORM: Book Cloth × Order Book fusion steered by the owner from the safer re-roll (candidates 2 and 3), approach B "Docked Slip"; seed:96c5a85ce2fec8e1. Signature interaction: the slip unfolds upward into the full sheet, tinted copies fanning beneath; adding an item makes the slip's line count tick. Motion grammar: paper-weight, short, eased, no bounce; reduced motion means instant.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
